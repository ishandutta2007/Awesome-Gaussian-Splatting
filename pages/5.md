# Dynamic & 4D Gaussian Splatting\n\nBy introducing the time dimension, 4D Gaussian Splatting captures dynamic and moving scenes. Time-varying deformations and velocities are modeled using additional neural fields or Fourier coefficients applied to the Gaussians.\n\n```mermaid\ngraph TD\n  A[Static 3D Gaussians] --> B[Deformation Network (t)]\n  B --> C[Shifted & Scaled Gaussians]\n  C --> D[Dynamic Scene Rendering]\n```\n
